The clean route

Roadmap to Platinum

Four passes through Leonida. Finish the story with the map open, then work outward from stranger strands to collectibles and the final 100% cleanup.

  1. 01Story

    Follow the gold thread

    12 trophies

    Start with Gone Fishing, then play the required jobs for Chapters 1–4 and finish the final mission. The six named mission trophies are Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child’s Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown and Operation Savage Glory.

    No story missables The campaign can be completed first and free roam continues after the final mission, so every story trophy can be cleaned up on the same save.

  2. 02People

    Finish the people of Leonida

    Strangers + activities

    Complete 8 stranger mission strands; the five finale trophies point to Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone. Sample all 9 activity types, then chase the longer activity goals: 42 Shooting Range golds, 14 drone race wins, 20 different race wins and 50 Challenges.

    Also in this pass Beat Cal’s fishing challenge, finish any arcade game, take Bret’s unique animal, and register 10 vehicles with Scooter Bros.

  3. 03Map

    Clear the map before you grind

    Collectibles + exploration

    Meditate at all 18 spots first and finish every zen animation. Then complete 3 Collectible Card sets, check in at 100 WAiNK locations, discover 100 vehicles and reveal every region for The Goodtime State.

    Spend as you go Buy every garage property and keep the shop spend moving toward $100,000 for Treat Yo Self.

  4. 04100%

    One last sweep for A Long Road

    Cleanup + Platinum

    Now clear the crime-and-count list: 250 stunts, 3 shops in one robbery chain, 5 bank vaults, the armored truck, the undetected jewelry job, one illegal operation per gang, a 4-star partner escape and five Combat Focus takedowns.

    Then close the loop Launder $250,000 in Marked Bills, finish the hidden one-offs, reach 100% completion and claim Vice Got a Price!.

Only missable flag
18

Positive Mental Attitude

Meditate at every one of the 18 spots across Leonida. Complete the full zen animation each time; if you leave early, that location can fail to register.

GTABase and PowerPyx flag this as the game’s only possibly missable trophy, so clear each meditation as you find it rather than saving them for post-story cleanup.

  • 01Stay through the breathing prompt.
  • 02Wait for the confirmation before driving away.
  • 03Tick the spot in this checklist immediately.
