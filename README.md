# Vice City Trophy Atlas — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Tracker

A fully offline trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5, covering the complete list of 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze, 28 of which are hidden on PlayStation. Each trophy carries its official name, description and grade, plus a step-by-step route written in our own words — what to do, where and how, and whether it can be missed.

## How to open it

The guide is static files with no build step, and it works with the network off — everything it loads lives under `app/`.

- Open `app/index.html` directly in a browser, or
- run `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080

## Features

- **Checklist stored in the browser** — mark trophies as you earn them; progress lives in localStorage and survives reloads.
- **Export / import** — save your progress to a JSON file and restore it in another browser.
- **Kind and grade filters** — story, strangers, collectibles, activities, crime, exploration and misc; Platinum, Gold, Silver and Bronze, plus earned/unearned and a text search.
- **Spoiler setting** — hidden story trophies stay masked (name, description, route and sources) until you earn them or switch spoilers off.
- **Roadmap to the Platinum** — four passes through Leonida: the story, stranger strands and activities, collectibles and exploration, then the 100% cleanup.
- **The one missable trophy flagged** — Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at all 18 spots) is the game's only possibly missable trophy, marked on its card, in the roadmap and in its own missable panel.

## Trophy-list sources

The official list — names, descriptions, grades and the hidden split — is the kit's official list, cross-checked against the published grade breakdowns (1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze; 28 hidden). The routes cite their sources next to each trophy, principally:

- Push Square — GTA 6 Trophy Guide and Roadmap
- GTABase — GTA 6 Trophy Guide
- PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide

Guide text is written in our own words; only the official trophy names and descriptions are reproduced as PlayStation publishes them.
